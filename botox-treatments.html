Anti-Wrinkle Treatment

Botox Treatments

Reduce the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles with our anti-wrinkle treatments. Using prescription-only botulinum toxin following consultation and prescribing where appropriate, Botox can soften expression lines while maintaining a natural appearance.

Popular treatment areas include the forehead, frown lines, crow’s feet, jaw slimming (masseter), chin, neck and facial contouring.

Every treatment plan is personalised following consultation, clinical assessment and a prescribing decision where required.
