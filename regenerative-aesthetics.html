PRP Facial Rejuvenation

+

PRP facial rejuvenation uses platelet-rich plasma prepared from your own blood as part of a personalised skin-rejuvenation protocol.

PRPFacial Rejuvenation

What this treatment may offer

  • Autologous blood-derived treatment
  • Skin-quality focused treatment planning
  • Can be combined with other appropriate skin procedures
  • Consultation-led protocol

Claims about expected changes in texture, lines or collagen should reflect the practitioner’s evidence and the individual treatment plan.

PRP Microneedling Facial

+

Sometimes described informally as a ‘vampire facial’, this treatment combines microneedling with PRP in a consultation-led skin-rejuvenation protocol.

PRPMicroneedling

What this treatment may offer

  • Combines microneedling and autologous PRP
  • Skin-texture focused treatment planning
  • Can be tailored to individual concerns
  • Non-surgical procedure

The informal nickname is included for clarity, while the clinical treatment name remains PRP microneedling facial.
