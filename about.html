Our Clinical Focus

Hair Restoration at the Centre of Our Care

Practitioner Profile

Meet Your Specialist

Zeeshan Akhtar

Hair Loss Specialist • Aesthetic Practitioner

Zeeshan Akhtar is an aesthetic practitioner with a specialist focus on hair loss and non-surgical hair restoration, particularly supporting clients experiencing hair thinning, reduced hair density and balding through personalised treatment approaches.

Every client begins with a thorough consultation to understand their concerns, assess the characteristics and pattern of hair loss, and determine whether treatment is appropriate and suitable for them.

Where appropriate, recognised hair-loss classification systems, including the Norwood and Ludwig scales, may be used to assess the pattern and extent of hair loss.

Where hair loss is complex, unexplained or presents with features that may require further medical investigation, appropriate referral to a dermatologist or relevant healthcare professional will be recommended.

Alongside hair restoration, Zeeshan offers a broad range of non-surgical aesthetic and regenerative treatments across the face, skin, hair and body.

With a strong emphasis on safety, responsible practice and natural-looking results, every treatment is tailored to the individual.
