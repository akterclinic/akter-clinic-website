Website Technologies

Cookie Policy

How cookies, embedded services and similar technologies may operate on the Akter Clinic website.

Last updated: 5 August 2026

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how Akter Clinic and third-party services used on this website may store or access information on your browser, device or terminal equipment. This includes cookies, local storage and similar technologies.

2. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files placed on a device. Similar technologies can include local storage, security tokens, pixels and scripts that remember settings, provide functionality, protect services or measure how a service is used.

3. Our current website setup

At the date of this review, the website's own code does not intentionally install first-party advertising cookies or Google Analytics. The website does, however, use or plan to use:

  • Cloudflare Pages or related Cloudflare security and delivery services;
  • an embedded Google Form for consultation requests;
  • an embedded Google Map on the contact page;
  • Google Fonts for typography; and
  • external telephone, email and Instagram links.

This list must be checked again immediately before launch and whenever a new plugin, script, analytics service, chat widget, advertising tool or embedded service is added.

5. Consent and exceptions

Where consent is required under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, non-essential storage or access technologies must not be activated before a visitor takes a clear positive action. Continuing to browse is not, by itself, valid consent.

UK law also contains limited exceptions for specified low-risk or necessary uses. Akter Clinic will document any exception it relies on and will still provide clear information and an appropriate way to object where the law requires it.

6. Google services

Google may receive technical information, including a page URL, IP address, browser or device information, when its embedded services load. Google may also use cookies, local storage and similar technologies for functionality, security, preferences and analytics.

Google's own privacy and cookie information applies to its services:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses cookies

7. Managing your choices

Once a consent mechanism is implemented, visitors will be able to accept or reject non-essential technologies and change their choices later through a visible cookie-settings control.

You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser. Blocking some technologies may prevent the Google Form, map or other third-party functions from working correctly.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when website technologies, suppliers or applicable law change. A new date will be shown at the top of the page.
