PRP Hair Restoration

+

PRP hair restoration uses platelet-rich plasma prepared from your own blood and may be included in a non-surgical hair-restoration plan for suitable people experiencing hair thinning.

PRPHair Restoration

What this treatment may offer

  • Uses an autologous blood-derived preparation
  • Non-surgical treatment option
  • Can be incorporated into a personalised hair plan
  • Suitable cases are selected following assessment

Expected response varies between individuals and results are not guaranteed.

PRF Hair Restoration

+

Platelet-rich fibrin is prepared from your own blood and can be discussed as a regenerative option within a personalised hair-restoration programme where clinically appropriate.

PRFRegenerative

What this treatment may offer

  • Autologous regenerative option
  • Consultation-led treatment planning
  • Can be used within hair-restoration protocols
  • Follow-up can be adapted to individual response

The practitioner will explain whether PRF is suitable and how it differs from other platelet-based approaches.
