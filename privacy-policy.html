2. Scope of this policy

This policy explains how Akter Clinic handles personal information collected through this website, the embedded consultation form, email, telephone, social media contact and other pre-consultation communications.

A separate clinical privacy notice may apply after you become a patient or client and the clinic begins maintaining treatment, consent, aftercare or medical records.

3. Information we may collect

  • Identity and contact information: name, email address, telephone number and contact preferences.
  • Enquiry information: treatment interest, preferred appointment date or time and the message submitted through the consultation form.
  • Health information: information about hair loss, skin concerns, medical history or treatment suitability if you voluntarily provide it. Health information is special category data and receives additional legal protection.
  • Appointment and communication records: correspondence, call notes and information needed to arrange or manage a consultation.
  • Images: photographs or before-and-after images only where separately provided and supported by appropriate permissions.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser, device, security logs and information generated when the website or third-party embeds are used.

Please do not send detailed medical information, identity documents or photographs through the initial Google consultation form unless the clinic specifically asks you to use an approved secure process.

5. Where information comes from

We normally collect information directly from you. We may also receive information from a person authorised to act for you, from a referring professional or from technical service providers that support the website and communications.

6. Who we may share information with

We may share personal information only where necessary and proportionate with:

  • authorised clinic practitioners and administrative staff;
  • Google, where Google Forms, Google Sheets or Google Maps are used;
  • Microsoft, where Outlook email is used;
  • Cloudflare and website hosting, security or technical support providers;
  • professional advisers, insurers, auditors and payment providers, where applicable;
  • regulators, courts, law-enforcement bodies or other authorities where disclosure is required or permitted by law; and
  • another healthcare professional where appropriate, authorised and necessary for care.

Akter Clinic should maintain appropriate contracts and access controls for processors handling personal information on its behalf.

7. International transfers

Some suppliers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where a restricted transfer occurs, Akter Clinic will use an applicable UK adequacy regulation, approved contractual safeguards or another lawful transfer mechanism, and will assess the relevant protections where required.

8. How long we keep information

Akter Clinic will keep information only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and to meet applicable legal, professional, insurance and dispute-management requirements.

9. Security

We use reasonable organisational and technical measures designed to protect personal information. These should include restricted access, strong passwords, multi-factor authentication, device security, secure deletion, staff confidentiality and regular reviews of who can access form responses and spreadsheets.

No internet or email system is completely secure. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information through ordinary email or the initial enquiry form.

10. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to:

  • receive information about how your data is used;
  • request access to your personal information;
  • ask for inaccurate information to be corrected;
  • request deletion in applicable circumstances;
  • request restriction of processing;
  • object to certain processing;
  • receive or transfer certain information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where processing relies on consent; and
  • receive safeguards relating to applicable automated decisions.

These rights are not absolute. Akter Clinic may need to verify your identity and may retain information where the law permits or requires it.
